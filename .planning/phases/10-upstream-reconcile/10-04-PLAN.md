`test/helpers/warn-guard.ts` (carried in verbatim by Plan 10-01's merge, already read in full
during planning): a global vitest `setupFiles` module. `beforeEach` spies on `logger.warn`
with a mock implementation; `afterEach` throws (failing the test) if any call fired without
being acknowledged. Opt-in shapes: `expectWarning(match?: string | RegExp)` — asserts at
least one `logger.warn` fired, optionally matching a substring/regex, and marks the test
acknowledged; `ackWarnings()` — acknowledges without asserting a specific message (escape
hatch for genuinely variable output; prefer `expectWarning` when the message is stable).
