Audit the 74-tool MCP surface for redundancy per decision D-02 (direct deletion allowed, no deprecation period). Purpose: D-02 explicitly allows one-pass tool deletion, but C-05 requires the audit, not a guess, to pick which tools. RESEARCH.md's Pitfall 6 warns that low usage is not the same signal as low security value; this plan's cross-check step exists specifically to prevent that mistake from reaching the cut list.
Output: 12-AUDIT-TOOLS.md in the phase directory.
